# PROJECT Amanda's Party Planner

## What is this?
A party planning app where a house party host can collaborate with invitees for bringing food and other equipment, make a shared Spotify playlist, and keep track of who was invited and who has confirmed attendence.

## DESIGN
- Light mode by default, with a toggle for switching between light/dark modes. Use Vuetify 3 library theme with Material Design icons where emojis make sense. 
- Grid design with cards for each app section, responsive and mobile-first with a full-width design.
- Have a countdown area that says "Months/weeks/days until the event"
- Have an area for notifications
- Have a profile section with user settings
- Have a task list with attendee assignments
- Have an area to add songs to the shared Spotify playlist
- Create any other sections you think would be useful to people attending a party.
